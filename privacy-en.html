This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how personal data of visitors to vinakovlab.ru (the "Site"), operated by Eduard Vinackov (the "Operator", "I"), is processed. By submitting the request form on the Site, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

Data submitted through the request form is used solely to contact you about your task and to prepare a reply or a proposal. The data is not used for automated decision-making and is not used for any purpose unrelated to handling your request.

The request form is technically processed via the third-party service Web3Forms (web3forms.com), which acts as a technical intermediary delivering the form data to the Operator's email. Data is not shared, sold, or used for any purpose unrelated to processing your request.

5. Retention period

Data is kept in the Operator's mailbox until the request is fulfilled and for a reasonable period afterward, for correspondence history, unless the user requests earlier deletion.

6. How to delete your data

To delete your personal data or withdraw your consent to its processing, email eduard.vinackov@yandex.ru with the subject "Data deletion" — the request will be fulfilled within a reasonable time.

7. Marketing communications

The Site does not send automated advertising or marketing messages. A reply to your request is personal and does not constitute a mailing. If a mailing list is introduced in the future, separate consent will be requested for it.
