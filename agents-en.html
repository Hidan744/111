01 — COMPANY DATA

Knowledge & Analytics

Internal Document Search

Problem

Policies, contracts, and manuals are scattered across folders and people's heads. New hires spend weeks figuring out "how things are done here."

Solution

The agent searches all company documents by meaning and answers with a link to the source.

Self-Service Analyst

Problem

There's no analyst on staff — the manager digs through Excel themselves to figure out why margin dropped or sales rose.

Solution

The agent connects to your ERP / CRM / warehouse system and builds the report you need from a plain-language question.

02 — PROCESSES

Operations & Risk

Document Workflow Automation

Problem

Manually filling out and cross-checking contracts, invoices, and acts eats hours and invites mistakes.

Solution

The agent fills out the document from a template, checks it for inconsistencies, and routes it for signature.

Compliance Agent

Problem

Small businesses rarely have an in-house compliance lawyer — violations only surface during an audit.

Solution

The agent checks documents and processes against regulations before the regulator does.

Incident Management

Problem

When something breaks or a complaint comes in, time gets lost just remembering how a similar case was handled before.

Solution

The agent logs the incident, finds similar past cases, and suggests a ready-made resolution.

03 — CONNECTION

Infrastructure

Agent + ERP/CRM Integration

Problem

The business already runs an ERP or CRM like amoCRM or Bitrix24 — but connecting an AI agent to it is technically hard, and that's usually what stalls adoption.

Solution

The agent gets access to the client's existing systems via MCP — no data migration, no process rework. One-time setup plus subscription support.

04 — NICHE INDUSTRIES

Industry Expertise

Industry Agents, Built on the Oil & Gas Model

Problem

Specialists — geologists, process engineers, technical staff — don't want a "generic chatbot" that doesn't understand their professional context.

Solution

The model proven in an oil & gas case gets ported to another technical industry: the agent already knows the terminology and the processes.

Product Hypothesis Testing

Problem

Product teams burn time manually collecting and structuring feedback instead of analyzing it.

Solution

The agent runs through the variants, gathers feedback from multiple sources, and delivers a verdict: hypothesis confirmed or not.
